Scope

When to use DevinTrace

  • Defects statable as a contract with an oracle
  • Weak tests, crash-on-bad-input bugs, scanner findings
  • Never auto-merges; not for vague feature work
  • Production needs: isolated runners, auth, a real queue

In a customer engagement

  • Week 1: the customer's scanner or Jira feed becomes the trigger
  • Pilot: 10 to 20 approved cases with agreed targets (proven-fix rate, time to proof, review time)
  • Then: grow the case library, run sessions in parallel, track provider-reported cost per proven fix
